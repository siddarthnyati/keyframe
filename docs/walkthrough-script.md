# Launch Desk walkthrough — 60-second voiceover

Six screens, one slate. Target under 200 words. Read at a normal pace; pause where the line breaks.

---

Hi, I'm Sid. This is Launch Desk, what a Prime Video producer would open on Monday to answer: is this title ready in every market, what is blocking it, and what do I send.

Today. One sentence per launch. The Terminal List season 2 launches in 12 markets in 14 days. Not ready. Three things are blocking it.

Requests. The intake queue. Each gets a suggested owner, a link to the board, and a reason. I confirm or change it. Nothing is re-typed.

Briefs. The creative brief beside what fans are saying this week, so the brief is written from evidence.

Launch board. Markets by deliverables, with owner, due date and state. Spec checks run on receipt, so Japan's missing 2:3 poster shows today, not at upload.

Markets. Where campaigns are live, launching or blocked. Click one for what each title still owes.

Updates. The Monday status and the vendor chase, drafted from the board. I edit and send. Nothing sends itself.

What I would not build: generative art, frame picking, variant choice, paid-media optimisation, auto-approval, auto-send, a listening product.

I would rather hear where launches go late today than show you more of this.
